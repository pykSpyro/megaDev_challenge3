Shared parser

Engineering ticket

The shared parser reads dates month first even when the tenant uses day-first dates. For example, report R-41200 from Harbour Dental describes 7 April becoming 4 July. Use the tenant’s locale, not the reporter’s browser locale, to establish the expected order. R-41205 belongs here even though intake tagged it hint: Morrow Tax uses fr-FR and booking-v2, and the report came through the app.

Engineering should make parsing match the tenant’s date convention, reject invalid dates, and test ambiguous dates through both app and email paths. Verify the final invite as well as the parsed value. The export identifies the behavior, not the parser implementation.

Booking-v3 hint

Engineering ticket

Booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants, while the form reads input in the tenant’s order. People who follow the hint can receive the wrong date. This is a separate UI and input-contract defect. Report R-41230 is one example: Copper Studio uses es-ES and booking-v3, the report came through the form, and intake incorrectly tagged it parser.

Engineering should derive the hint and parsing from the same tenant date convention. Test Alder Physio (de-DE), Copper Studio (es-ES), and Pale Harbour (pt-PT), with browser locales that differ from the tenant. Check that day-first booking-v2 forms and non-form paths remain correctly handled. Preserve the user’s intended date in the invite.
